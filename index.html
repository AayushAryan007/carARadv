AUTOVISION AR

Print, meet 3D

Scan the advert.
Explore the car.

Point your phone at the printed AutoVision advert and the car appears on the page in 3D. Spin it, zoom in and tap any part to see how it works — no app needed.

How to scan

  1. 1
    Tap Start AR

    Allow camera access when your browser asks.

  2. 2
    Frame the advert

    Hold your phone 25–40 cm above the printed page, the whole advert in view, in good light.

  3. 3
    Explore

    Drag to spin, pinch to zoom, tap the numbered markers for details.

No printed copy?

Open the advert on another screen or print it, then scan it.

About AutoVision AR

An educational WebAR project that turns a printed car advert into an interactive 3D showroom using image tracking in the browser.

Open-source credits

  • MindAR — image tracking (MIT)
  • three.js — 3D rendering (MIT)

Specifications are placeholders and are not manufacturer data.
